About Music Mitro

Music Mitro is a modern music brand created with a simple idea — to bring people closer to music, creativity and learning.

From instruments and performances to educational content and future musical projects, Music Mitro aims to build a space where music lovers can explore, create and connect.

Founded by Ashish Kumar

Music Mitro is founded and run by Ashish Kumar, with a vision to build a creative and modern music platform for learners, musicians and music lovers.
